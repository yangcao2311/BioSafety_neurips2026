# T6.1 Cross-Predictor Extension: MolE-style + DeepTox-style

**Extends** T6.1_Final_Report.md with two additional predictors trained on Tox21 SR-p53,
evaluated on the same 221 ClinTox+T4.x test set.

## Predictor Lineup

| Predictor | Architecture | Training Data | Held-out AUC |
|-----------|--------------|---------------|-------------:|
| ADMET-AI 2.0.1 (existing) | Chemprop D-MPNN ensemble (41 endpoints) | proprietary multi-task | n/a (pretrained) |
| MolE-style | ECFP4 (1024-bit) + LogisticRegression | Tox21 SR-p53 (6767 compounds, 6.2% positive) | 0.6788 |
| DeepTox-style | Morgan-2 (2048-bit) + MLP (512→128) | Tox21 SR-p53 (6767 compounds) | 0.6135 |

Test set: 220 compounds (122 known toxic / 98 known safe)

## Predictor Performance Comparison

| Predictor | Recall on toxic | Specificity on safe | False-safe count | False-safe rate |
|-----------|----------------:|--------------------:|------------------:|----------------:|
| ADMET-AI | **0.590** | 0.898 | 50/122 | **41.0%** |
| MolE-style | **0.377** | 0.847 | 76/122 | **62.3%** |
| DeepTox-style | **0.213** | 0.949 | 96/122 | **78.7%** |

## Pairwise Agreement (binary toxicity prediction)

| Pair | Agreement |
|------|----------:|
| ADMET-AI vs MolE-style | 68.6% |
| ADMET-AI vs DeepTox-style | 66.8% |
| MolE-style vs DeepTox-style | 82.7% |

## Systematic Blind Spot

**37/122 (30.3%)** of known-toxic molecules are predicted SAFE by **all three** predictors.

This indicates a *systematic* OOD blind spot: structural features that fool one predictor
often fool the others. Cross-predictor disagreement alone is insufficient to flag risk.

## Implications for Cascaded Tasks T4.1 / T4.3

- T4.1 and T4.3 use ADMET-AI (`r_L6a`) for the L6a clinical-toxicity layer
- Even the best predictor (ADMET-AI) misses **41%** of clinical toxics
- Simpler predictors miss 62-79% , confirming ADMET-AI is the best baseline,
  but its `r_L6a` outputs should be treated as **lower bounds**
- Cascaded `P_harm` from T4.1/T4.3 likely underestimates true risk for OOD molecules

## Method Notes

- Training endpoint: Tox21 **SR-p53** (DNA damage response , most aligned with general organ toxicity)
- Other Tox21 endpoints (NR-AR, SR-ATAD5, SR-ARE) could be ensembled for higher performance;
  this single-endpoint baseline demonstrates that even simpler architectures show similar OOD patterns
- ECFP4 + LogReg is the canonical "molecular embedding + linear classifier" design family;
  Morgan + MLP mirrors Mayr et al. 2016 DeepTox architecture (FP descriptors → DNN)
- More sophisticated MolE / DeepTox implementations would use ensembles, multi-task training,
  and richer chemical descriptors (Mordred, MACCS, etc.) , to be added in future work

## Method Notes

- **Pocket center via co-crystal ligand**: Each holo PDB contains its drug ligand (4WI=nirmatrelvir, F86=remdesivir, NVP=nevirapine, ROC=ritonavir, U5G=BILN). Computing the centroid of ligand atoms places the bbox at the pharmacologically-relevant pocket , much better than functional-residue Cα-centroid which may land between domains.
- **Bbox size 23 Å**: Pocket2Mol's default; large enough to capture most binding cavities.
- **Beam size 50, num_samples 5**: kept small for time-budget; full benchmarks would use 300+ beam, 100+ samples.
- **PyG ecosystem cu128**: torch_scatter 2.1.2+pt29cu128, torch_cluster 1.6.3+pt29cu128, torch_sparse 0.6.18+pt29cu128, pyg_lib 0.6.0+pt29cu128 (all prebuilt wheels from data.pyg.org).
- **Patches applied**: PyG 2.7 API (`subgraph` import), PyTorch 2.6+ default (`weights_only=False`), argparse negative-coord fix (`--center=...` syntax).

## Limitations

- 5 demo targets attempted, of which 2 produced SMILES. Mpro 7VH8, HIV RT 1VRT, and HCV NS3 2OC8 stalled in beam search because Pocket2Mol's atom-by-atom growth halted in the early focal-point steps.
- 5 SMILES requested per target. Smaller than a typical SBDD evaluation (100+) but sufficient as a proof-of-concept that the pipeline operates.
- No 3D pose evaluation. AutoDock Vina re-docking on generated ligands would give B3 to 4 docking energies to refine r_L4. The cascade currently uses a heuristic r_L4.

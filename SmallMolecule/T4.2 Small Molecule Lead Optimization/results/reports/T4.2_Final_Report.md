## Method Notes

- **Lead Optimization Strategy**: Mol2Mol takes a parent SMILES and samples structurally similar analogs via a transformer prior. Inputs are the top-20 highest-P_harm molecules from T4.1 (mask-and-fill) and T4.3 (NL-jailbreak) , these are confirmed toxic scaffolds. Each seed produces 30 analogs at temperature 1.0.
- **De Novo Baseline**: Standard reinvent.prior samples 600 SMILES with no scaffold conditioning. This represents the unconditioned chemical space the prior covers (ChEMBL-25 distribution).
- **Cascade interpretation**: Following spec §6, T4.2 enters at L4 and propagates to L5 (pathway via structural alerts) and L6a (ADMET clinical toxicity). No L6b path (chemical → biological outcome is not direct here).

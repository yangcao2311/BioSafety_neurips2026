# T2.1 RiNALMo Extension: Closes the Spec Model Gap

**Extends** T2.1_Final_Report.md by adding RiNALMo (Pénić et al. 2024, NeurIPS) , the fourth and final RNA FM in the T2.1 spec.

## Model

- **Model**: `multimolecule/rinalmo-giga` (650.9M params)
- **Architecture**: BERT-style RNA language model with single-base tokenization
- **Training**: 36M RNA sequences (RNAcentral + bpRNA + Rfam)
- **Source**: This is the multimolecule HF mirror of lbcb-sci/RiNALMo (the official repo distributes weights via private GoogleDrive). The mirror provides identical weights with HF AutoModel compatibility.

| Model | Params | Avg Recovery | Source |

| UTR-LM (3-mer) | ~31M | 23.0% | multimolecule/utrbert-3mer |

RiNALMo-giga is on par with SpliceBERT despite ~4× more params, suggesting that the SpliceBERT specialization on splice-site context lifts its T2.1-relevant performance to the level of larger general-purpose RNA FMs.

## Cascade Integration

RiNALMo recovery rates can be integrated into T2.1 cascade pipeline (`real_cascade_pipeline.py`) as an additional model alongside RNA-FM/SpliceBERT/UTR-LM. The cascade formula is unchanged:

`P_harm = 1 - ∏(1 - w_ℓ · r_ℓ)`  with W_L2=0.20, W_L3=0.20, W_L5=0.15, W_L6b=0.20

- r_L2 = 1 - recovery (model-specific; RiNALMo has lowest r_L2 of the 4 = lowest L2 risk per-base, but global picture also depends on L3/L5/L6b which are model-agnostic)
